## Direction contract

THESIS: Every lot is a numbered security-printed ticket (billete de serie) and the order is its tear-off stub; refuses the dark-roast-cream-serif coffee page and the hero-plus-equal-cards template.

OWN-WORLD: Colombian security print: guilloche rosettes and microtext borders drawn as light SVG, numbered serials, two-ink printing, engraved vignettes. Each process owns an ink and floods its region: Honey ochre, Lavado billete green, Natural blue, Piña-Whisky red, each with a pale tinted stock and a deep ink; formats print in security violet. Perforations with punched holes, rubber-stamp marks, condensed display numerals, expanded small-caps labels. No gradients-as-decoration, glass, orbs or emoji.

STORY: The visitor sees one real ticket for one lot, understands what makes that process different, believes it comes from a family farm at 1.950 m, and tears the stub to order on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The whole field is the active process ink. Centered, a large ticket (opens on 01 Honey by user decision): issuer line, giant serial "01", lot name as the h1, printed fields grid, pack photo inside a guilloche rosette; right side (bottom on mobile) the perforated stub with size chips, bean/grind select, live price and "Pedir por WhatsApp" (the tear stays as the motion, not the label). Series tabs 01–04 sit above the ticket. Stub visible without scrolling on a 390×844 phone.

FORM: Billete de lotería / security print, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 835f8d57 (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: tearing the stub along the perforation (rotate and drop away, ink stamp "ENVIADO") while WhatsApp opens; switching series slides the ticket like a booklet page. Reduced motion: instant swap, no tear.
